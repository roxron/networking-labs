# Task 03.003 — MPLS TE tunnels

[Main lab page](../../../README.md) · [MPLS task group](../README.md)

**Status: planned.** No tunnel configurations or reservations have been created.

## Intended objective

Build and test TE tunnels after the infrastructure stage is verified. Define headends and tailends, tunnel identifiers, explicit or dynamic paths, reservation bandwidth, priorities, and traffic-steering behavior before writing per-router configurations.

For each future tunnel, document its path and purpose in the diagrams, explain how traffic enters it, verify the established path and reservations, and test behavior under a link failure. Record observed results separately from expected behavior.

Future tasks may add alternate paths, protection, additional ISP transport instances, and inter-AS CsC. Their scope will be decided in subsequent lab stages.
